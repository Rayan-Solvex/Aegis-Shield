Aegis Shield: Economic Privacy Protocol

Aegis Shield combines Umbra-compatible privacy primitives with application-layer mechanism design. The goal is not just private transfers, but economically secure private transfers where honest auditing is profitable and privacy violations are costly.

Protocol Thesis

Umbra provides privacy primitives. Aegis provides the governance and incentive layer. The combined system aims to make selective disclosure safe enough for regulated use without turning privacy into a trust exercise.

Identity Model

Roles are determined by cryptographic capability, not by username. A sender should only have sender powers. A receiver should control claim and disclosure powers. An auditor should only gain access through a user-issued grant and a staked registry identity.

Economic Security

The compliance stake model is the admission bond for auditors. It is the key design move that turns privacy from a UI promise into a system of aligned incentives.
