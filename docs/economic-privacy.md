# Economic Privacy

Economic privacy means privacy backed by incentives.

## Threat Model

A purely technical privacy system can still fail socially if:
- fake auditors can appear at zero cost
- receivers cannot distinguish honest auditors from malicious ones
- grant revocation has no economic consequence

## Aegis Response

Aegis introduces a bonded auditor model.

### Admission

Only registered, staked auditors can receive Aegis-designated disclosure flows.

### Profit Model

Honest auditors earn:
- audit service fees
- repeat grant flow from users
- cumulative reputation

### Misbehavior Cost

Dishonest auditors risk:
- slash of compliance stake
- registry suspension
- permanent reputation damage
- exclusion from future grant flows

## Privacy + Incentives

Umbra ensures that unauthorized parties cannot decrypt private data.
Aegis ensures that authorized parties have capital at risk if they misuse the role they were granted.

That combination is the foundation of economic privacy.
